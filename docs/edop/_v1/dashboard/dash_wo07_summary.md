# dash WO-07 summary: conservative span gate, and a look at the edge

2026-11-17. Spec `dash_wo07_conservative-span-gate.md`; record `dash_wo07_results.md`. Branch `dashboard`. Nothing deployed. The API, payload and Sandbox page are unchanged; the engine's HYDE query was reshaped at Karl's request after the edge look (below).

- **Items 1 and 2, gate and warning (Settlements side, `5369ca6`).** `edops_span_gate.js` counts the HYDE steps inside the entered span (the table is the database's `hyde_times`, checked by a test) and compares them with a limit by scope: single basin and ring 12 (legibility), buffer 9 (delivery, as a polity until measured). The warning is a yellow placeholder line under the Band T year inputs, shown on leaving a field or pressing Enter, and re-checked on scope, level and Band T changes. It never blocks. 8 tests; the dashboard folder is 112 passed. The Polities side is not built (see the polity finding).
- **Item 3, the edge, settlements.** Single basin (Lviv, Santa Fe) and Lviv ring load in 2 to 3 s at every span tried, 12 steps to 56. HYDE's table fits the pane at 12 and 13 steps (891 px of 907, values wrapping) and overflows at 56 (3,242 px). 13 steps is where the display runs out, as Karl saw; the delivery limit is far above it for single basins.
- **Item 3, the edge, polities: the span is not typed.** The Polities side's Band T year inputs are hidden (also in the Sandbox), so a polity's span is its slice's own dates. Cliopatria has 15,690 slices; the median is 6 years; 330 (2.1%) have more than 9 HYDE steps; the 8 example polities have 0 or 1. The exposed slices are modern states with annual HYDE steps and large areas, and they are slow today: **China 2000-2023 (24 steps) 104 s, Brazil 1987-2023 (37) 206 s, Argentina 1936-2023 (75) 193 s, USSR 1953-1968 (16, 22 million km2) over 5 minutes** (the page run timed out). For China, band T alone takes 101 s; bands A-E take 3.3 s. Canada 1949-2023 (74 steps, 9.9 million km2) was not run; the model says it is worse.
- **What that means.** A polity gate cannot sit under a year input. It has to act on the slice (steps and area). The 9-step rule would flag the 330 slices but ignores area; the biggest slices are slow even at few steps. A user who picked a modern state waited minutes; that led to the polity gate and the query reshape below.
- **Also found.** A polity link ignores `from_year` and `to_year` (the slice's span wins), so a Copy link for a polity does not carry the span. Every manual slice step (Next, Prev, First, slider, picking a polity) also sends a full `/api/areas` request, Band T included, only to repaint the map from the member basin ids (Play steps skip it).
- **Polity gate, built after the edge look (Karl: ceiling 5 s; halt on the slice; disable Get signature with the reason; unticking T re-enables it).** The slice list already carries `area_km2` (within about 1% of the geometry). The page estimates Band T time as cells (area / 73 km2) x (35 microseconds + 2.1 per HYDE step); over 5 s, Get signature is disabled with "Band T for this slice would take about N s. Untick T to continue without it.", and the map's background request leaves Band T out. 6 tests. Argentina (1936-2023) took three times as long as Northern Song at the same cells and steps: cost depends on the polygon's outline as well as its area, so an estimate cannot promise the ceiling; the gate is a stopgap and a server time limit would be the guarantee.
- **The HYDE query was reshaped** (`9340d31`, Karl: fix the query first): one row per cell with each variable's array sliced to the span's steps, the overlap computed once. Rows identical to the old query (one `p90` differs by 2 parts per million, from cell order, which also changes `p90` when the old data is shuffled); engine and baseline tests pass (217). Northern Song 1000-1999 63.6 s to 7.0 s; China 2000-2023 about 100 s to 10.2 s; China 1950-1952 13.5 s to 4.7 s. Canada-sized slices are still over 5 s (Python builds a histogram per step and variable over 135,000 cells). The gate's numbers are fitted to the new query.
