# dash WO-06 summary: time series, preliminary questions

2026-11-17. Spec `dash_wo06_time-series-prelim-stuff.md`; record `dash_wo06_results.md`. Measurement and code reading only; no app, engine, API or database change. Scratch scripts `scripts/edop/dashboard/dash_wo06_*.py` (not committed yet). Local server and local database; the server may differ (see results).

- **Item 3, chosen variables on the engine path.** No variable filter and no lean form exist. `detail` is applied at serialization, so every histogram is computed and then dropped. The spread (p10, p90) is only in `detail`, not in the lean row. Source selection already exists (`permitted`). Per-cell values are mostly there (LMR and GEDA hold a cells x years matrix; HYDE returns a row per cell per step). The public `/api/signature` takes a span only for `scope=basin` (older methods); `buffer` and `area` require one year. `/api/areas` takes ranges but is internal.
- **Item 2, the polity jump.** It is HYDE, not span length. For a polity, HYDE is 95% of Band T time (LMR 0.2 s, GEDA 0.7 s, eVolv2k nothing at 1,000 years). HYDE's SQL recomputes each cell's overlap with the polygon once per cell and step, so cost is cells x HYDE steps (about 15 to 20 microseconds a pair). Steps are 1 in 100 years from 1000 CE, 17 in 800 years from 1000, 80 in 800 years from 1200 and 82 in 1,000 years, because HYDE steps are centennial to 1700, decadal to 1950 and annual after. Hence 63 s at 1,000 years for Northern Song (37,901 cells), 6 s for the Seljuk Dynasty (4,825) and 2 s for the Kingdom of Croatia (1,596). **Fixable:** forcing the overlap once per cell (one word) cuts the polity cost by 40 to 50% with identical numbers; the real fix is one row per cell with array slices (as LMR and GEDA do), unmeasured.
- **Item 1, cost of a lean request.** Partly answered. The four sources together take about 0.3 s for a single basin at 1,000 years, but the whole API call takes 3.1 s; the remaining 1 to 1.5 s lies outside the Band T functions and was not traced. Whether `detail=false` helps time (it shrinks the payload) was not measured.
- **Item 4, span threshold (proposal, conservative first).** A dynamic gate on the Signature tab's year inputs that counts HYDE steps in the span (the 128 step years are a small known table) and years for LMR and GEDA. Polity: about 9 HYDE steps or fewer (as built, about 5 s for a large polity). Single basin: no delivery limit; the display decides (the HYDE table is 82 columns wide at 1,000 years). Ring and buffer were not measured; a large buffer may behave like a polity. A size-aware gate (budget divided by polity size) is possible later.
- **Two limits, not one (Karl).** Delivery time is measured here; what the interface does with a large result (display, readability) is a design matter and was not measured beyond WO-05's render times. The governor is the lower of the two, by scope.
- **Beyond the WO, from the discussion:** conservative gate first; redesign how HYDE data displays (lines, not a column per step) and look at it; revisit the gate after that and after deciding whether to reshape the HYDE query.
