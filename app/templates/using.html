Using EDOPS

EDOPS describes the environment of a place, past and present, from global datasets. These worked examples show it supporting reasoning about history. The same data is also available as raw numbers for computational work, and as a source for other tools.

Societies: for 1,291 societies coded in the Ethnographic Atlas, how a trait such as subsistence or belief in high gods relates to the environment of the place.
