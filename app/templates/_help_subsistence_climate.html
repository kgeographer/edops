Every subsistence-coded society is plotted by aridity index (log scale, Arid to Wet) against mean annual temperature (Cool to Warm) — the two axes an earlier PCoA comparison (WO8a) found best separated subsistence modes: cold foragers cluster upper-left, warm-dry pastoralists lower-left, agriculture holds the wet-mild quadrant. Societies coded with the selected value are highlighted against the full subsistence-coded set shown in gray.

Because those regions were predicted in advance rather than found by looking, a highlighted group landing where its subsistence mode was expected to land is a real (if modest) confirmation, not a pattern search after the fact — unlike High gods' Environment scan, which has no such prior prediction to check against.

The composition donut resolves each society's language family via Glottolog and is hover-linked to the scatter and the map: hovering a family highlights only that family's societies everywhere at once. Families below 20 societies in the current selection are marked "small n" in the donut legend; "Unresolved" covers societies Glottolog could not match to a family.

What this does not show. A highlighted cluster landing in its predicted region is consistent with the environmental hook, but consistency isn't proof — shared ancestry or geographic contact among related societies could produce the same visual clustering even where the prediction happens to hold. The hover-by-family probe is a visual check, not the family-restricted permutation control used elsewhere in EDOPS's statistical work.
