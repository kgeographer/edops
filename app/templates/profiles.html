    This is a sample of the environmental profile a place portal could carry. Each profile was generated ahead of time, by fixed rules, from global datasets; no one wrote it by hand and no language model wrote it. The text of each statement comes from a template, and the rules choose which statements are true of the place.

    How to read a profile. Setting describes the place itself. The other sections describe the drainage basin that contains it, and say so. A statement appears only where the data support it; silence is deliberate.

    Sources: HydroSHEDS (BasinATLAS, HydroRIVERS), One Earth Bioregions, Natural Earth, OpenStreetMap and Wikipedia.

    Each line the rules chose is shown as written in the template file, with its {slots} unfilled; the small label is the line's key.
