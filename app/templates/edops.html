About the project

The Environmental Dimensions of Place Service (EDOPS) project assembles and delivers environmental data profiles ("signatures") for any terrestrial Earth location via an Application Programming Interface (API) and in the three pages of this web platform developed so far: Sandbox, Data Explorer, and Workbench. The features and functionality of these are described in the project documentation.

EDOPS is one component of Computing Place, a broader independent research program undertaken in 2026 by geographer Karl Grossner, PhD. EDOPS is a work-in-progress (now at v0.4). It was conceived as a data instrument supporting the work of its nascent sister component, Cultural Dimensions of Place (CDOP): investigating correspondences between cultural practices and environmental settings. It has became evident as the project has progressd that EDOPS could made be more broadly useful by extending its initial cultural geography remit to historical and social science research more generally, and to teaching.

While Computing Place is a long-standing personal research initiative, EDOPS is no longer a strictly independent project—the Institute for Spatial History Innovation (ISHI) at the University of Pittsburgh is now a welcome development partner.

Environmental Dimensions of Place Service (EDOPS)

EDOPS generates structured environmental signatures for any location on Earth. Signatures are derived from global datasets aggregated at the hydrological sub-basin level — capturing physiography, hydrology, climate, and coastality — with optional historical climate enrichment from paleoclimate reconstructions. EDOPS is the environmental component of a broader Computing Place research initiative.
