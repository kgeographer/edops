About the project

The Environmental Dimensions of Place Service (EDOPS) project assembles and delivers environmental data profiles ("signatures") for any terrestrial Earth location via an Application Programming Interface (API) and in the three pages of this web platform developed so far: Sandbox, Workbench, and Data Explorer. The features and functionality of these are described in the project documentation.

EDOPS is one component of Computing Place, a broader independent research program undertaken in 2026 by geographer Karl Grossner, PhD. EDOPS is a work-in-progress (now at v0.4). It was conceived as a data instrument supporting the work of a nascent sister component, Cultural Dimensions of Place (CDOP): investigating correspondences between cultural practices and environmental settings. As the project has progressed it has become evident that EDOPS could made be more broadly useful by extending its initial cultural geography remit to historical and social science research more generally, and to teaching.

While Computing Place is a long-standing personal research initiative, EDOPS is no longer a strictly independent project—the Institute for Spatial History Innovation (ISHI) at the University of Pittsburgh is now a welcome development partner.

Environmental Dimensions of Place Service (EDOPS)

EDOPS generates structured environmental profiles ("signatures") for any location on Earth, derived from global datasets aggregated to hydrological basins at multiple scales.

Signature composition is spatially and thematically flexible. A request can name a settlement — resolved to its containing basin or to a multi-basin neighborhood — or a polity or regional boundary at a given date, resolved to the set of basins it encloses.

Signatures can include any combination of variables, grouped as bands: physiography, hydrology, climate, coastality, with optional historical enrichment from paleoclimate and land-use reconstructions.
