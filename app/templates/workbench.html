D-PLACE Societies

1,291 ethnographically documented societies from D-PLACE

D-PLACE (Database of Places, Language, Culture, and Environment) provides coded observations for 94 anthropological variables across subsistence strategies, settlement patterns, kinship systems, and social organization. Focal years are typically 1850–1940, representing the "ethnographic present." Of these societies, 87% have been spatially joined to EDOP's environmental signatures, enabling exploration of environment-culture relationships. 158 (13%) lack basin assignments (islands, coastal locations). Initial marker colors differentiate OneEarth Bioregions.

Dominant Subsistence (EA042) and High Gods (EA034) below are two exemplar traits from the Ethnographic Atlas dataset, chosen for their differing potential association with environmental settings.

African Regions in Historical Perspective

              web site
            

            
This tab overlays the 34 pre-colonial African subregions proposed by
              Lovejoy et al. (2021) as a controlled vocabulary for linking open-source
              historical data. Select a region to read the article's rationale for its extent and
              the peoples associated with it. The regions were drawn for organising data rather than
              for environmental analysis; the Workbench places them over EDOPS basin signatures to
              ask how far each one holds together as an environmental unit.
